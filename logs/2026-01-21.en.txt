Detail record: The image reveals striking detail in its grand spiral arm structure. Bright young star clusters are distributed along the spiral arms, whose radiation and stellar winds shape a complex network of dust filaments and bubbles. These structures are exceptionally clear in the infrared band, highlighting obscured star-forming regions.

Evolution inference: The dynamics of the central bar dominate the galaxy's evolutionary process, with its gravitational funnel effect continuously supplying material for starburst activity. Observational data support the hypothesis of the key role of barred spiral structure in galaxy evolution.
