[Log Entry 3298] Deep Space Union Observer DKSan3, recording complete.

Target: Dark Wolf Nebula (Sandqvist–Lindroos 17), coordinates: Scorpius, RA 16h 32m, Dec -25d 34m, embedded within the Gum 55 (RCW 113) nebula. Resembling a dark wolf, crouching in an ocean of interstellar dust.

Observation conclusion: This is a typical dark nebula structure, whose essence is highly condensed interstellar dust and molecular gas. It absorbs the intense ultraviolet and visible light from young stars within Gum 55 and re-radiates it as longer-wavelength infrared. This process is critical, as it effectively prevents high-energy light from heating the gas in this region. When the gas temperature is maintained at a sufficiently low critical point, gravity has the opportunity to take over, thereby collapsing to form new stars. For astronomers, dust is usually an obstacle to observation, but here, it is a catalyst for stellar birth.

System attrition report: DSU-7 sensor array, when processing the low-illumination background of this dark nebula, exhibited a 4.3% thermal noise drift in the long-wave infrared filter, which has been calibrated and compensated within allowable range. Additionally, the primary mirror coating, after sustained bombardment by high-energy particles in the Scorpius region, has suffered a 0.7% decline in reflectivity; in-situ regeneration is recommended during the next maintenance cycle. Mission data package has been compressed, encoded, and prepared for transmission to the deep space network.
