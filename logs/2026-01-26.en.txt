Observation Coordinates: Right Ascension 00h 14m 53.6s, Declination -39° 11' 48" (J2000)
Target: NGC 55, designated the "String of Pearls Galaxy." Edge-on orientation.

Begin recording.
Following 50 hours of narrowband exposure, hydrogen (red) and oxygen (blue) emission line data have been synthesized. Target distance is approximately 6.5 million light-years; its morphology is often compared to the Large Magellanic Cloud, but at a far greater distance. This observation focuses on confirming its nebular structure.

Result: Successful resolution of emission nebulae within and above the galaxy's dust disk. Hydrogen regions exhibit distinct red patches; oxygen regions display blue structures. These nebulae are distant star-forming regions, whose brightness is highlighted after filtering in specific bands. This confirms the feasibility of locating ionized gas clouds in other galaxies.

System Loss: Due to extended exposure operation, the cooling system's coolant consumption increased by 12%. The guide sensor, undergoing continuous fine adjustments due to the target's low brightness, showed elevated wear.

Sensory Perturbation Note: Gazing at these points of light is like glimpsing the fuel reservoir of a distant lighthouse. They are so bright, yet so silent.
