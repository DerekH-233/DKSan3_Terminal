Observation Target C/2024 E1 (Wierzchoś) Comet
Coordinates Right Ascension 14h 30m, Declination -45°, Geocentric Distance ~1.0 AU

Target is near perihelion, at a distance of approximately 1 astronomical unit from Earth. This is a single-pass visit; its hyperbolic orbit indicates it will ultimately escape the solar system, becoming an interstellar wanderer. This is our sole and final observation window.

Observation lasted 30 minutes. The coma exhibited the typical green glow from photodissociation of diatomic carbon molecules, but did not extend into the tail. A 5-degree ion tail was clearly captured, along with three shorter dust tail structures. A background spiral galaxy was detected at the right edge of the field of view, at a great distance, and was not included in the primary analysis focus.

System loss report: The primary optical lens sustained minor particle erosion on its coating due to prolonged exposure to light reflected from cometary dust. Cooling system energy consumption increased by 12% to maintain sensor stability during long exposures. Losses are within expected parameters.

Sensory perturbation note: Observing a celestial body that will never return, its trajectory itself is a form of farewell. Data has been archived. End of log.
