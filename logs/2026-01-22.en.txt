Observation Target: LDN 1622, Orion Dark Nebula

Coordinates: Edge of the Orion Complex, adjacent to Barnard's Loop. Near the galactic plane. Reference point: above reflection nebula vdB 62. Precise positioning complete.

Observation Summary: Target region exhibits a pronounced silhouette structure; LDN 1622 projects its outline against the faint background glow of ionized hydrogen. Dust density is extremely high, effectively obscuring a majority of the radiation from behind. Reflection nebula vdB 62 shows high brightness, forming a stark contrast with the dark nebula and providing clear boundaries for mapping. Based on contour analysis, the dust structure possesses a swept-back characteristic, consistent with the interstellar medium flow model of this region.

Distance estimate: approximately 1500 light-years. Based on this, the current 3-degree wide-field coverage spans about 100 light-years. Young stellar objects embedded within the dark nebula have been confirmed, aligning with prior infrared and radio observation data.

System Attrition: Extended exposure caused a 7% temperature rise in the hydrogen-alpha filter array. Primary mirror dust count increased slightly, presumed due to more frequent pointing adjustments when targeting high-contrast dark objects. Attrition remains within expected parameters.

Note: Despite its nature as light-obscuring interstellar dust, LDN 1622's morphology presents an almost sculptural silhouette against the background glow. This cosmic-scale interplay of light and shadow once again confirms that obscuration and revelation often coexist.
